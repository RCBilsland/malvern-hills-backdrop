Each line is a real elevation profile from the Environment Agency's 1 m LIDAR terrain model, crossing the ridge at 45 degrees to the line through its summits. The lines are 200 m apart and sampled every 20 m within a circle 14.8 km across, centred on the midpoint of the summit line, between Worcestershire Beacon and Herefordshire Beacon. Heights are exaggerated three times, as in the layered view, and flatten towards the rim of the circle.
